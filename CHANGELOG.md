# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [0.3.13] - 2026-10-10

### Added
- Data sources shown in `/gli probe db sources`
- First version: profession spell tooltips, one options tab per profession
- Fishing: skill with lure, buff and equipment bonus, profession tier and game statistics
- Fishing: casts, catches and fish are recorded in Glimpse: Database and shown in the tooltip
- Fishing: cast with a key and double right click, equips the rod and applies the best lure
- Fishing Buddy and Better Fishing support (tooltip only)
- `/gli prof` commands
- New addon icon (addon list, README, CurseForge)
- Fishing: Weather-Beaten Journal learned or not in the tooltip, Find Fish switched on at login (optional)

### Changed
- WoW Forever only (interface 16001)
- Fishing: the double click only casts while standing still and out of combat
- Fishing: the double click runs through the double-click dispatcher of Glimpse, mouse button selectable (needs Glimpse 0.3.7)

### Fixed
- Buff and lure bonus read wrong numbers from color codes and multi-line tooltips
